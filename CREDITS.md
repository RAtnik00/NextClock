## FlexClock

NextClock includes adaptations from FlexClock by tr3tol, notably the settings
layout, and follows its digit-pack conventions. FlexClock also informed the
modern private-interface adapter. These contributions must not be represented
as wholly original NextClock work.

- Original project: https://github.com/tr3tol/FlexClock
- Author: https://github.com/tr3tol
- Source license: GNU General Public License version 3; see LICENSE.
- Reference inspected on 2026-11-04: d83bab703d5ea2b99c4cbbc574a1ad53b43e4d55.
  This identifies the audited reference, not necessarily the original input revision.

NextClock modifications by RAtnik00 include earlier-iOS adapters, image and
archive validation, charging-transition handling, localization, preview and
favorites. Their availability depends on the NextClock version. This attribution
was clarified on 2026-11-04. Existing upstream notices must remain intact.
